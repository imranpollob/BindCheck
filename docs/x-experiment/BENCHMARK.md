# Mutation benchmark for proof-to-execution binding (lane-x)

Date: 2026-10-28. Package: `benchmarks/binding-mutants/`. Raw evidence:
`results/raw/X-BM/` (ignored). Exploratory development evidence; no prior
report or verdict is changed. All verdicts herein are build/reachability
observations, not D1 security verdicts (all UNKNOWN at that level).

## 1. Objective

Give the main lanes a ready-made, compiling, ground-truthed mutant set for
evaluating automated discovery (contract §11-a): 30 binding mutants over
two already-exposed development applications (WarpToad 18, Payy 12),
covering circuit and consumer sides and every operator class in tonight's
brief: guard removal, unconstrained public input, public→private swap,
root/anchor swap, fee/recipient/amount unbinding, dropped nullifier write,
word misorder — plus dead-path activation and decoder offset.

## 2. Construction

- Baselines vendored from pinned sources (`baselines/`, hashes in
  `manifest.json`): WarpToad `973e7d1e…`, Payy `c4d2c90…`.
- Mutants generated by exact-match edits (`make_mutants.py`, fail-closed
  on count mismatch); two reuse frozen evidence (WT-C03 = M-DOM/D1P-15,
  WT-S01 = M-SRC/D1P-19) instead of re-proving.
- Toolchain pins: nargo beta.22 (WarpToad), nargo beta.14 (Payy),
  solc 0.8.29 (WarpToad consumer), solc 0.8.20 + via-IR/optimizer-200
  (Payy consumer, mirrors pinned hardhat config). All runs `nice -n 19`.
- Third-party dependencies are NOT committed: copied from read-only
  reference paths at build time (paths + versions in `manifest.json`).
  Payy upgradeable contracts use compile-only stubs (`_stubs/`), clearly
  marked never-deploy; everything else links real vendored-equivalent sources.

## 3. Build results

Full build 2026-10-28, load average ~42 (a parallel lane was running
heavy jobs; recorded in `results/raw/X-BM/logs/load.txt`). Command:
`OUT=<abs path> sh benchmarks/binding-mutants/build.sh` (absolute OUT
required; a relative-OUT run failed on log redirects — rerun guard added).

- Compile: **30/30 pass** (9 WT-C nargo beta.22, 9 WT-S solc 0.8.29,
  7 PY-C nargo beta.14, 5 PY-S solc 0.8.20 via-IR/optimizer-200).
- Honest `nargo execute` (frozen D1P-15 witness): C01, C02, C04, C05,
  C08, C09 pass (honest behavior preserved); C06/C07 fail with
  `Cannot satisfy constraint` — predicted, honest-breaking by design;
  C03 skipped by design (new struct field needs an Aztec-branch witness;
  covered by the reused D1P-15 real-proof precedent).
- Bonus signal: the beta.22 compiler's own unused-variable lint names
  the freed public word for C01 (`nullifier`), C02 (`max_fee`),
  C08 (`recipient_address`) — consistent with the X1 finding that this
  is the only built-in signal, and it states no mismatch.
- Per-mutant exits, artifact hashes, and log tails: `manifest.json`
  `observed` fields; raw logs under `results/raw/X-BM/logs/`.

## 4. Reachability (cheap)

- WarpToad circuit mutants: honest-path `nargo execute` with the frozen
  D1P-15 honest witness (same-root EVM branch). Pass = honest behavior
  preserved. WT-C06/C07 are honest-breaking by design (recorded).
  WT-C03 needs an Aztec-branch witness; covered by the reused D1P-15
  real-proof precedent, not by the cheap check.
- Payy circuit mutants: compile + structural diff only (no 2-proof
  witness harness in this benchmark — see §7).
- Consumer mutants: solc compile + diff audit (guard/effect lines).
  No EDR execution per mutant in this round.

## 5. Ground-truth table

Per-mutant operator, broken binding, and predicted effect are in
`manifest.json` and `mutants/<ID>.md`. Predicted effects are UNEXECUTED
except for the two reused precedents; they are detection targets, not findings.

## 6. Excluded candidates (with reason)

- Private-recipient WarpToad circuit (changing `recipient_address` to
  private): breaks the 11-word verifier/consumer interface arity — a
  system-level non-compiler, not a binding mutant. Excluded as
  non-compiling at composition level.
- Removing `assert(fee_factor != 1)` / `assert(priority_fee != 0)`:
  policy-neutral hygiene asserts with no proof-to-execution consequence
  identified; excluded as no-effect (would be negative controls, not targets).
- Brillig/unconstrained-fn mutants: no Brillig in either circuit's
  binding path; out of scope.
- Payy `agg_final` forwarder mutants: the forwarder copies words
  unchanged (T16); its mutant space collapses to misorder, already
  covered at both ends (PY-C07/WT-C09 class). Deferred, not excluded.

## 7. Limitations and next steps

- No per-mutant witness/proof/replay: concretization harness per mutant
  (especially Payy 2-proof witnesses and consumer EDR replays) is the
  expensive step and is NOT done here.
- Consumer reachability is structural, not executed.
- Suggested next: (i) honest-mint EDR replay across consumer mutants
  (predict S05 honest-break); (ii) Payy Prover.toml harness for one
  padding + one mint record reused across PY-C mutants; (iii) run the
  X2 derivation + nargo-check baseline over all 30 and score detection.
