# D2 Gate A early-comparison protocol draft v1

**Date:** 2 November 2026

**Status:** `DRAFT_UNSEALED_DO_NOT_SCORE`

**Research verdict:** `UNKNOWN`

**Machine control:** `protocols/d2/d2-05-early-comparison-draft-v1.json`

This document specifies the construction inputs that Steps 6a and 6b must
return to Step 7. It does not authorize a scored run. The case manifest stays
`DRAFT` until exact cases, policies, controls, tools, transformations, forms,
and output rules are complete and content-sealed by Step 7.

## 1. Diagnostic purpose

Gate A asks a narrow question on exposed development material: under explicit
information budgets, does BindCheck add a predeclared relationship diagnosis
or validation capability beyond credible one-sided and simple alternatives?
It does not estimate prevalence, precision, recall, transfer, security,
generality, or novelty.

The primary outcomes are fixed before construction:

1. target detection at E1 or higher for each eligible case and arm with an
   output-independent, provenance-recorded development label;
2. coverage of predeclared producer relation, decoded identity, consumer
   effect/state, property, and direction fields;
3. highest E0–E4 evidence level and automatic/assisted/manual status for each
   common validation stage;
4. false warnings only on controls positively verified for the exact target
   property, plus separate adjudication of every other report; and
5. human, agent, machine, setup, specification, scoring, correction,
   validation, adjudication, and elapsed effort.

Warning wording, repair suggestions, runtime profiles, secondary warnings,
and new mechanism hypotheses are exploratory. They cannot rescue a failed
primary comparison.

## 2. Development cases and provenance

The candidates are deliberately exposed. The D2-01 49-row reconciliation
table is not a sample size: it repeats cases across a 30-mutant benchmark,
nine ablation rows, eight role targets, and two exact Payy rows.

| group | purpose | label provenance | control boundary |
|---|---|---|---|
| `GA-PAYY-TAIL` | Earlier/check-added message-tail contrast | public disclosure; T15–T19 historical E4; D1P-42 exact mapping; D2-01 current E1 | check-added is a repair contrast for the exact tail property, not global safety |
| `GA-WARPTOAD-MDOM` | injected producer application-domain mismatch | D1P-15/19/21A mutation provenance; D1P-30 development E4 | pinned is honest for the exercised relationship, not a proven negative |
| `GA-WARPTOAD-MSRC` | injected consumer source-state mismatch | D1P-19/21A/21B mutation provenance; D1P-30 development E4 | same limitation |
| `GA-WARPTOAD-RELATIONSHIP` | distinguish one-sided, union, structural, simple, and full relationship analysis | authored mutation operators; D1P-43 frozen associations; D2-01 association ledger | E8 remains `UNKNOWN`; E9 is a matching honest control, not safety evidence |

Step 7 must select exact nonduplicated case IDs and bind producer, decoder or
bridge, key/verifier, consumer, state, policy, seed, and validation-route
bytes. Each row records family lineage and exposure. Revisions,
transformations, and warning objects do not create independent families.

No target label may come from the output being scored. Acceptable sources are
a primary disclosure plus artifact evidence, an authored mutation plus a
checked semantic control, historical E2–E4 evidence with its original
automation/exposure status, or a property-specific repair/safe-control
validation. These are output-independent provenance records, not necessarily
independent human labels. Existing E1 names such as `effect-authorization`
are diagnostic names, not human authorization judgments.

## 3. Policy boundary and human dependencies

A human policy author must derive `sigma` from primary project sources without
seeing scored outputs. A different human reviews every permission, obligation,
effect, transformation, admitted state, and environmental assumption. Actual
prior exposure and overlapping project roles are disclosed. If a policy is
unresolved, that row remains in construction/applicability accounting as
`UNRESOLVED`; it does not enter an authorization/coverage detection
denominator or block unrelated objectively scoreable rows.

Policy compatibility covers identity, asset, beneficiary, domain, quantity,
state or version, activation, and discharge where applicable. Proof occurrence
or matching role names alone cannot establish authorization. Generic
invariants run only when primary target sources justify them; WarpToad
solvency, for example, remains excluded unless its intended backing policy is
documented.

Structured matches should be deterministic. Ambiguous localization,
diagnosis, and non-target reports require two independent humans working from
randomized tool-neutral packets. A distinct third human adjudicates only after
original scores are sealed. AI may normalize records and run checks, but it is
not a policy reviewer, independent scorer, or adjudicator. Missing human staff
leaves affected subjective rows unresolved; Step 7 may restrict objective
scoring to rows whose policies and targets are completely frozen.

## 4. Required comparison arms

### Step 6a: internal alternatives and transformations

Step 6a returns six runnable arms:

- producer-only;
- consumer-only;
- their unjoined report union;
- basic structural composition with exact public-input mapping and explicit
  occurrence/dataflow checks;
- strong simple composition using the same conditional facts, identities,
  policy, annotations, assumptions, and bounds as full BindCheck, implemented
  as straightforward propagation, identity joins, and direct P/O/E checks;
  and
- the frozen full BindCheck arm.

Basic structural output is a structural candidate, not an authorization
failure. The strong-simple and full arms must have an auditable equal-
information matrix. Useful facts may not be withheld from the simple arm.

Step 6a also constructs seeded identifier renaming, helper extraction,
equivalent-expression rewriting, and matched public-input reordering with
producer-consumer correspondence preserved. Each accepted transformation
needs its original, mapping, exact bytes/hashes, build result, a transformation
argument, and appropriate semantic/proof/execution controls. Compilation or
existing tests alone are insufficient. Rejected transformations and failures
stay in the ledger. Stability is compared by semantic identity, never line
number.

### Step 6b: policies and external comparators

Step 6b returns:

- a direct same-policy property harness wherever `sigma` is expressible;
- pinned Noir compiler/default checks;
- an applicability record for ZKAP, CCC-Check, CIVER, SmartAxe, Slither,
  Mythril, Foundry invariants, Echidna, Picus, and ELSA; and
- runnable generic/default and same-policy arms for every applicable tool with
  a property interface.

Native, adapted, unsupported, unavailable, and not-applicable statuses are
kept distinct. Frontend mismatch is not an algorithmic miss. An adaptation is
labeled and charged; it does not inherit the external tool's name as though it
were native. Default arms use documented defaults plus at most one
prospectively declared non-default setting. Same-policy arms receive the same
policy and admitted states. Every candidate gets the same E2–E4 validation
route and budget.

An LLM baseline is supplementary. It runs only if exact model, context,
prompts, stochastic settings, run count, token/cost cap, and scoring are frozen
before output. Its absence cannot block the core comparison.

## 5. Matching and evidence grading

Step 7 freezes a semantic target for every positive. A target identifies the
case/revision, producer relation or allowed one-sided locus, decoded public
identity or index set, consumer effect/state/obligation locus, property and
direction, and minimum evidence level. A detection must cover every mandatory
field. Several reports may jointly cover a target only when the frozen target
allows union coverage; all required indices or identities must then be
covered.

Reports are clustered by tool, arm, case, rule family, semantic locus,
effect/property, and evidence identity. Warning ordinal and source line do not
create findings. Missing locations or diagnoses remain missing rather than
being supplied by normalization.

Every other report is retained and independently classified as a false alarm,
real out-of-scope issue, new candidate, or unresolved. New candidates do not
enter the frozen target denominator. They receive the common validation budget
and responsible-disclosure handling.

Evidence retains the research contract ladder:

- E0: no target report;
- E1: warning or abstract candidate;
- E2: a model satisfying the actual compiled producer relation, decoder,
  consumer model, and policy violation;
- E3: a real proof accepted by the pinned variant-specific verifier; and
- E4: that proof accepted by the unchanged variant-specific consumer from an
  admitted or reachable state, with the incompatible committed effect or
  undischarged obligation checked on state.

All tools get detection credit at their actual level. Only E4 is an end-to-end
validated mismatch. Historical E4 can establish a development label but does
not become automatic validation by a current arm.

## 6. Outcome classes and denominators

An eligible completed positive is `DETECTED` when all mandatory fields match,
otherwise `MISSED`. `UNSUPPORTED` names a documented frontend or semantic
gate. `UNRESOLVED` covers timeouts, crashes, resource stops, incomplete output,
or unresolved policy/scoring. `NOT_APPLICABLE` means that the arm has no
relevant interface by design. `BUILD_FAILED` remains a construction outcome.
Only the first two occur in a target-performance denominator.

Separate denominators are mandatory:

| denominator | includes |
|---|---|
| construction | every planned case-arm, failed build, and rejected transformation |
| applicability | every finalized case-arm partitioned by eligibility/status |
| target detection | eligible, completed, positively established targets only |
| diagnosis | detected reports for which diagnosis fields were required |
| safe-control false warnings | only positively verified controls for that exact property |
| non-target reports | every normalized report, before and after duplicate clustering |
| validation | unique candidates, not warning objects |
| transformation stability | successfully constructed, semantically controlled variants |
| effort | every session and attempt, including failures and corrections |

Unsupported and unresolved rows remain visible outside the supported
denominator. Silence is never safety. Honest fixtures and repair contrasts do
not enter a false-warning denominator without positive property-specific
justification.

This is a purposive two-lineage development comparison. Report exact case
tables, numerators, denominators, and missingness. Do not report p-values,
confidence intervals, pooled precision/recall, or prevalence. Stochastic tools
use frozen seeds and expose every seed result; repeats are not independent
cases.

## 7. Resources, retries, and effort

Draft caps are 1,800 seconds per deterministic/static/symbolic case-arm and
3,600 seconds aggregate per same-policy or stochastic-fuzzer case-arm across
three frozen seeds. A baseline never gets less analysis time than full
BindCheck on the same case. Per unique candidate, E2 modeling gets 1,800
seconds, E3 proof generation 3,600, and E4 replay 1,800. Each process is
capped at 16,384 MiB, scored parallelism is one, and scored execution makes no
network calls. Step 7 must demonstrate how these limits are enforced.

Construction is capped at 24 researcher-hours and scoring/adjudication at
eight. Setup and proof generation are separated from analysis caps but remain
part of total cost. There are zero scientific retries. A single separately
labeled infrastructure recovery is possible only before any scored output and
only with identical sealed bytes/configuration; the failed attempt is
preserved.

Effort records active human minutes by role and category, agent activity plus
human inspection, and exact machine command/configuration, tool identity,
host, seed, CPU/memory/elapsed observations, exit, and output identity. Shared
and arm-specific effort remain separate. A count of manual steps is a proxy,
not measured time saved.

## 8. Step 7 seal

Step 7 may run the experiment only after one timestamped manifest binds:

- exact cases, lineages, exposures, inclusions/exclusions, labels, policies,
  controls, transformations, failures, files, sizes, and hashes;
- every arm's information budget, implementation, version, configuration,
  bounds, seed, native/adapted status, and resource cap;
- warning targets, duplicate and union rules, randomized scorer packets,
  human roles/exposures, forms, and adjudication process;
- candidate identity and equal E2–E4 routes;
- environment, runner, dependency, network, stop/retry, output-namespace, and
  completion checks; and
- hashes of the blank case, measurement, effort, and scoring forms.

A missing case-specific policy or human score marks only the affected row
`UNRESOLVED`; unrelated rows with frozen policies and wholly objective matching
may proceed. A missing global manifest, matching framework, arm information
budget, or resource-enforcement method blocks the whole scored run. Partial
outputs are retained as `UNRESOLVED`; no result is overwritten.

## 9. Diagnostic decision rule

`SUPPORTED_ADVANTAGE` requires at least one predeclared relationship target on
which full BindCheck gives a valid transformation-stable diagnosis or higher
evidence grade absent from strong simple composition and every applicable
equal-information comparator. The difference cannot depend on extra facts,
frontend inapplicability, a false safe-control report, or a post-output rule.
This supports only a bounded exposed-case statement; the overall capability
and novelty claims remain open.

`INCONCLUSIVE` covers missing discriminating cases, unresolved policy/control/
scoring, unsupported or timed-out credible arms, ties that do not validate the
intended capability, or an information-budget confound. It permits prospective
infrastructure repair or a narrower question, not threshold tuning.

`CURRENT_CLAIM_CONTRADICTED` applies when strong simple composition or another
applicable equal-information method matches or exceeds full BindCheck on every
eligible relationship target/evidence grade, or when the apparent advantage
depends on bug-specific information or disappears under transformations and
controls. The contribution must then narrow or stop; there is no automatic SoK
pivot.

One bounded Steps 8–10 prototype is justified only if a trace identifies a
concrete generalizable missing mechanism. Before editing, freeze that one
mechanism, primary test, controls, success/failure criterion, and at most two
researcher-days plus eight machine-hours. Two missed defects by themselves are
not a mechanism, and the prototype cannot retroactively rescore Gate A.
