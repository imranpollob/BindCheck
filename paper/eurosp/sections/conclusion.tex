\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}

A valid proof certifies a statement, not an effect. We defined binding as the
requirement that every value deciding an effect follows from the statement or
is left open by the intended policy, classified its violations into five
patterns that mirror classic signature misuse, and showed that they make up
87 core auditor-reported findings, 78\% of the rated ones Critical or High.
Neither the circuit nor the contract alone carries the specification;
\tool{} reconstructs it as two explicit ledgers. \DEMO{With proof-system
knowledge and a second look, its final version finds
10 of 14 held-out audited flaws and 31 of 37 held-out mutants, and it
uncovered three new flaws.} Binding should be checked as routinely as a
signature.
