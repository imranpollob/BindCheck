\section{Ethics, Disclosure, AI Use and Open Science}\label{sec:ethics}

\para{Ethics.} All exploit reproduction ran on local Anvil/Hardhat chains we
controlled, with synthetic identities and assets, from source exported at
pinned commits. We sent no transaction to any public network and did not call
any deployed contract. The census read public Sourcify exports and performed
read-only code and balance queries; it inferred no consumer links. No human
subjects were involved.%

\para{Use of generative AI.} Generative AI was used as a research method and
in preparing this paper, and we disclose it in full. AI coding agents
implemented most of \tool{}, the regression controls, the synthetic benchmark
cases and their expected labels, the baseline arms, the validation harnesses
and the analysis scripts; drafted the application policies for the case
studies; screened literature; classified verifiers; proposed recall-set and
maturity labels; and drafted this manuscript. We used them to scale artifact
handling across thousands of repositories and to write many small,
precisely specified fixtures. Every AI-produced label is reported as AI
opinion and is excluded from any quantity we present as human-validated;
expected outcomes were committed before the runs they score; and every
number in this paper is traced to the script and file that produced it.
