\section{Benchmark Design}\label{sec:benchmark}

A binding benchmark must pin all three artifacts of every case, include a
legitimate counterpart for every flaw so that false alarms are measurable, and
keep development cases separate from held-out ones. Existing ZK bug
collections satisfy none of these for the integration layer: zkbugs locates 0
of its 140 entries in Solidity~\cite{zkbugs}, the SoK's 13 integration cases
are narrative~\cite{sok-snarks}, and ELSA's ``ZKP'' set consists of classic
SWC faults injected into ZKP-project contracts~\cite{elsa}.%
% src: RELATED-WORK.md#6a (framing 2 support bullets); reports/related/evidence/zkbugs-census.txt; reports/related/evidence/elsa-artifact-inventory.txt
\
Table~\ref{tab:benchmark} summarizes our benchmark's components.

\para{Paired synthetic cases.} Each of 23 families contains one vulnerable
and one legitimate case that differ in the binding property only; each case
ships a Circom circuit, a verifier, a consumer and a policy file. The families
cover recipient and amount binding, native value, burn ownership, state writes,
ERC-721 safe-mint recipients, guard dominance (irrelevant branch, guard after
effect), replay-key binding, nesting and position canonicality, chain and
contract nullifier scope, commitment order and fields, verifier arity,
decoding order, prefix-versus-full arrays, overlapping masks, array-length
signals and field-alias decoding. Expectations for every tool arm were frozen
before any tool was implemented.%
% src: reports/redteam/REPORT.md#synthetic-comparison (23 vulnerable / 23 legitimate; family list); redteam/adversarial/expected.json#frozen_before_implementation=true
\
The cases and labels were authored with an AI assistant and are development
fixtures; human review of every label is

\para{Regression controls.} 122 analyzer controls (24 legacy and 98 analyzer
fixtures) and 18 adversarial soundness cases encode the expected verdict and
reason for each recognizer, including the negatives that motivated each
positive-evidence rule. The frozen analyzer passes all of them.%
% src: reports/soundness/REPORT-2.md#verification-and-exact-provenance (122 controls, 18 soundness cases); docs/FREEZE-2026-11-09.md (34 tests OK)
\
They were written with an AI assistant and are not human-validated.%
% src: reports/groundtruth/CLAIMS.md#A30

\para{Development corpus (DEV).} 56 repository revisions: 50 open-source
Circom/Solidity applications selected for coverage during feasibility work,
plus three historical vulnerable/fixed pairs (zkDrops, Semaphore, Payy). DEV is
a convenience selection, used for tuning; no DEV number is a prevalence or
transfer estimate.%
% src: reports/groundtruth/CLAIMS.md#A24; reports/soundness/REPORT-2.md#full-dev-ledger (56 rows)

\para{Recall inventory and gate set.} From audit reports, advisories, issues
and fix commits we inventoried 47 reported defects; 27 fall in scope under the
v2 strict-integration rule (20 under the earlier v1 rule). The 16 v2-strict
cases with resolved vulnerable and fixed commits form the gate set. 5 of the
16 pairs are \emph{exposed}---their repositories were inspected during
development---and are reported separately; 2 overlap a sampled held-out
family.%
% src: reports/groundtruth/REPORT.md#2-recall-set-counting-rule (47; 27 v2; 20 v1); #5-gate-manifest (16; EXPOSED 5; overlap 2)
\
Proposals from source reports alone map 8 of the 16 pairs to no P1--P5
predicate; we keep them, because a benchmark that drops what a tool cannot
express overstates recall.%
% src: reports/groundtruth/REPORT.md#5-gate-manifest (proposed expected pattern NONE: 8)
\
Expected pattern and location for each pair await human review: all 16 rows
are currently \textsf{UNREVIEWED}.%

\para{Held-out sample (EVAL).} A source inventory yielded 5,024 repository
revisions, 56 of them DEV and 4,968 EVAL, grouped into 1,384 eligible EVAL
families that share no repository with DEV.%
% src: reports/groundtruth/CLAIMS.md#A20
\
We stratified families by the strongest maturity label of any member
(verified-deployed, deployed-address-in-docs, audited-production,
hackathon-student, unclassified; populations 38, 303, 210, 82 and 751 under
the sampler's rule) and drew up to 60 per stratum by a preregistered hash
order, giving 278 families.%
% src: reports/groundtruth/CLAIMS.md#A21 (sampler populations 38/303/210/82/751); dataset/EVAL-SAMPLE-PLAN-v2.md (hash rule, quota 60, frozen 2026-11-04)
% src: reports/groundtruth/REPORT.md#7-maturity-audit-of-the-278-sampled-families (278)
\
Maturity labels are keyword heuristics: for the audited-production stratum,
the preregistered README rubric agreed with the label for 4 of 60 sampled
families, and all 4 were false on inspection.%
% src: reports/groundtruth/CLAIMS.md#A22; reports/groundtruth/maturity-summary.json
\
We therefore report results per stratum and never weight by stratum
population before the labels are human-reviewed
\MISSING{human maturity review of 278 families, review/maturity}.

\para{Deployed verifiers.} A passive census of Sourcify exact-match
verifiers on seven chains (snapshot 2026-11-03) found 1,049 deployment rows,
1,027 unique deployments and 561 unique runtime hashes; a separate join matched
277 consumer deployments to 86 GitHub repositories. No transaction was sent and
no deployed contract was called.%
