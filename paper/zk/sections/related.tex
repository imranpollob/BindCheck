\section{Related Work}\label{sec:related}

\para{Which value does the proof constrain?}
ZKAP builds a dependence graph from Circom source. Its unused-public-input
detector asks whether a declared public input participates in the circuit's
constraints~\cite{zkap}. Consider a consumer that chooses a beneficiary after
checking a proof. Every declared public input could be constrained, yet the
beneficiary could be absent from the vector. Checking that property requires
the consumer's effect and the vector it supplied to the verifier.
Circomspect inspects circuit source for patterns such as underconstrained
assignments and non-strict binary conversions~\cite{circomspect}. Picus
checks whether the compiled constraints determine an output uniquely for
given inputs~\cite{picus}. A unique output still leaves open which public
position the consumer reads for that output. These tools supply evidence
about the producer side of the interface.

\para{Evidence from audits.}
The Railgun audit traces a missing commitment to the construction of the
public-input hash~\cite{abdk-railgun}. The Anon Aadhaar audit compares the
order of circuit outputs with the arguments supplied by the
contract~\cite{pse-anonaadhaar}. Both diagnoses identify the specific
correspondence an automated check must recover. Our audit census groups
published diagnoses by that correspondence and records whether the circuit
was needed (Table~\ref{tab:class-census}). Audit selection and reviewer
judgment limit inference about prevalence.

\para{Integration and comparison.}
The SNARK security SoK already identifies integration as a distinct layer,
documents zkDrops, and calls for joint Circom and Solidity
testing~\cite{sok-snarks}. Slither supplies contract-side data flow for our
consumer baseline~\cite{slither}. Some unbound effect arguments can be
identified in Solidity alone. To attribute a result to circuit reasoning,
our evaluation asks whether withholding the circuit changes the decision
(\S\ref{sec:res-ablation}). The ZKAP arm remains unavailable in the recorded
comparison (\S\ref{sec:method}).
