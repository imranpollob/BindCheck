\section{Related Work}\label{sec:related}

\para{Studies and benchmarks.} The SNARK SoK~\cite{sok-snarks} defines the
integration layer, counts 13 of 141 vulnerabilities there, gives the zkDrops
alias as an example, and proposes combined Circom/Solidity testing as future
work; we take its problem statement as given and contribute a benchmark,
a checker and a measurement. A 2026 evaluation of six circuit tools on 70
Circom bugs defers integration~\cite{zkp-tools-study}. zkbugs~\cite{zkbugs}
(140 entries, 0 in Solidity) and the 0xPARC tracker~\cite{zkbugtracker} are
circuit- and narrative-oriented respectively.%
% src: RELATED-WORK.md#32 (SoK, tools study, zkbugs, 0xPARC rows)

\para{Circuit analysis.} ZKAP~\cite{zkap} detects circuit vulnerability
patterns including unused public inputs; Picus~\cite{picus} decides output
uniqueness; Circomspect~\cite{circomspect} lints; zkFuzz~\cite{zkfuzz} and
zkCraft~\cite{zkcraft} find under- and over-constraint by mutation;
CCC-Check~\cite{ccccheck} and Zequal~\cite{zequal} check computation--constraint
consistency; CIVER~\cite{civer} and Coda~\cite{coda} verify circuit
specifications. All take only the circuit as input. ZKAP's unused-public-input
pattern is complementary to P1: it sees a public input the circuit ignores,
whereas P1 concerns an effect value that never enters the public vector.%
% src: RELATED-WORK.md#31 (tool rows); #6c item 4

\para{Contract analysis.} Slither~\cite{slither} and LLM-assisted analyzers
see only Solidity. ELSA~\cite{elsa} targets ``ZKP-based smart contracts'' but
reads no circuit file and evaluates seven injected SWC classes. SmartAxe~\cite{smartaxe}
is the closest cross-component analog, checking paired bridge contracts.%
% src: RELATED-WORK.md#31 (ELSA, SmartAxe rows)

\para{Verifier configuration.} The default-key class was exploited against
Veil Cash and FoomCash in 2026~\cite{veil-poc,foomcash}, and a public scanner
reported it against all 20 verifiers of another project~\cite{zeto-186};
current snarkjs templates add per-input field checks~\cite{snarkjs}. P4 is
therefore prior art that we include for completeness, not a contribution.%
% src: RELATED-WORK.md#31 (zkjac scanner row: 20 Zeto verifiers); #4 (P4 row)

\para{Toolchains and rollups.} Fuzzing of ZK compilation
pipelines~\cite{circuzz} and of proving toolchains~\cite{lietome}, formal
models of rollup contracts~\cite{rollup-formal}, and zkEVM free-input
checking~\cite{freever} address adjacent layers.

\para{Search method.} Our priority statement rests on a bounded search
(November 2026) of arXiv, IACR ePrint, DBLP and venue pages, audit-firm sites
and LLM-audit benchmarks, plus primary-source inspection of the ELSA artifact
and zkbugs.%
