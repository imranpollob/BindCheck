\section{Evaluation Methodology}\label{sec:method}

We ask four questions. \textbf{Q1:} How do single-layer tools, naive
compositions and \tool{} behave on paired cases---how often do they decide,
warn, and certify? \textbf{Q2:} Does circuit reasoning change any \tool{}
verdict? \textbf{Q3:} What do the tools report on a preregistered held-out
sample and on known vulnerable/fixed pairs? \textbf{Q4:} What does a passive
census of deployed verifiers show? We make no claim that any tool is
``better''; we measure what each one can and cannot tell an analyst.%

\para{Scoring.} A \emph{detection} requires the right pattern and the right
location: the emitted code must address the case's property and its location
must match the expected \code{path:line} pattern. A \emph{false alarm} is a
warning on a repaired revision or on a legitimate case. An \emph{unsafe
certification} is a \VWS{} (or a tool's equivalent ``safe'') on a vulnerable
case or revision. \UNK{}, timeouts, build failures and unavailable tools are
neither detections nor misses; we report them in their own columns, and a
tool that does not support an input is never charged with a miss. On the
synthetic set, detections are upper bounds: any violation by any pattern counts
until code-level relevance is adjudicated, for every arm alike.%
% src: reports/redteam/REPORT.md#synthetic-comparison (upper-bound rule); eval/common.py (pattern_locations, path:line)
\
We report, per tool, the \emph{decided rate} (non-\UNK{} outcomes over all
cases) and \emph{warning precision} (warnings on vulnerable cases over all
warnings); the latter is computed against AI-authored labels and is a
screening statistic, not a precision estimate.

\para{Preregistration.} The synthetic expectations were frozen before
implementation; the held-out sampling rule, quota and hash order were frozen
on 2026-11-04 before any held-out analysis; and the held-out harness refuses
to run unless annotated dataset and analyzer tags match the ledger and
analyzer hashes. Held-out analysis is at most once: an interrupted or failed
acquisition becomes \UNK{} and is never retried with hints or larger
budgets.%
% src: redteam/adversarial/expected.json#frozen_before_implementation; dataset/EVAL-SAMPLE-PLAN-v2.md; eval/README.md
\
Recall on the gate pairs runs only after human reviewers record, for each
pair, the expected predicate and vulnerable-revision location; these labels
may not be derived from any tool output. Exposed pairs are reported
separately from unexposed ones.%

random order that show the code at the location, the matched verifiers'

any prior label. Two raters fill identical sheets independently; we report
Cohen's $\kappa$ and resolve disagreements by discussion. The 16 development
warning groups have cards prepared; adjudication itself is
