was evaluated on author-built mutants of two development projects. Its
results---including a same-policy Foundry harness that detected 8 of 9
resolved mutants where the static analyzer detected 5 of 9, and zero supported
transfers in 12 attempts on 8 further projects---are a different analyzer on different
inputs and are not pooled with any result in this paper.%
