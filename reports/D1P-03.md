## Corrected consumer and producer semantics

Pinned `RollupV1` does not have the producer grammar previously summarized in
the integration plan. Its scan length is configurable per registered verifier.
After the three header public inputs, consumer kind 0 advances one word, kind
2 reads/consumes four words, kind 3 reads/consumes five, and every other kind
including 4 reverts. Solidity bounds behavior also makes a truncated kind-2
or kind-3 record revert when the helper reads past calldata.

The frozen producer walk is different. For an active recursive frame it treats
kinds 2, 3, and 4 as 4-, 5-, and 5-word record starts, and a different kind
ends export. A record that starts before the N=8 boundary can be emitted through
word 7 even when the logical record would require 9 or 10 words. Inactive frames
emit nothing. The new protocol states these as separate relations. It does not
infer policy from either behavior.

## Track A protocol

The [new protocol](../docs/d1-paper/d1p-03-protocol.md) names the six exact
D1P-02 query hashes and defines `C` accurately: it is the selected legacy
backward ACIR cone, **not the full producer relation**. It specifies four
separate diagnostics:

- goal-only `G`;
- selected producer-cone-only `C`;
- exact historical combined `C ∧ G`; and
- `C ∧ G ∧ cell` over the 25 soundly specialized cells.

Expected SAT/UNSAT directions are explicitly labeled hypotheses. The expected
combined direction is SAT for non-vacuity and UNSAT for prefix/tail for both
repaired idioms, but the only actual historical combined observations remain
six timeouts/UNKNOWN. The older v3-derived goal-only timeout and later v5
specification-only SAT diagnostics remain distinct.

Per stream the five cells are inactive, active-empty, length 4, length 5, and
truncated/observed length 8. Crossing two streams gives 25 cells. The protocol
forbids a cell specialization from changing selected ACIR constraints, the
goal, or message values.

## Track B protocol

Track B remains unimplemented. The protocol specifies:

- separate occurrence multisets P and O from active, certified logical frames,
  with reviewed V2 immediate-settlement policy and `(frame, offset)` identity;
- E from committed actual consumer effects only: the spent mint-registry write
  or a successful exact-token burn transfer, with all effects discarded on
  outer revert;
- exact multiplicity and semantic-key matching, low-160 recipient decoding,
  and no splitting, aggregation, deferral, or substitution;
- direct `agg_agg`-to-`agg_final` forwarding of roots, commit hash, and all
  messages through the recursive public-input vector, followed by the outer
  three-header-plus-messages consumer mapping; and
- actual consumer kind handling, registry/token state, later guards, verifier
  boundary, calldata bounds, and atomic rollback.

It retains V2's funded standard OpenZeppelin 5.0.1 token family, amount and
recipient restrictions, initial unspent registry, and empty substitutions.
These are declared scope restrictions, not implementation constraints added to
hide a defect. P/O remains analyst policy: source/implementation behavior does
not independently establish what must be permitted or obligatory.

The evidence boundary is explicit. Solver SAT plus `nargo execute` is a
concrete model, and an abstract-verifier consumer run is not a real proof.
Real-proof replay requires a revision/key-matched `agg_final` proof accepted by
the actual verifier and unchanged consumer with committed state effects. A
concretely validated result may carry V2's bounded VIOLATION category at
concrete-model grade, but it cannot be described as end-to-end or satisfy the
central real-proof gate. The available historical proof bundles are N=1000;
no N=8 real-proof bundle exists.
