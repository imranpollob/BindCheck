## 1. Outcome and limits

D1P-21A passes the two requested campaign gates without running a solver
campaign. Four newly generated real UltraHonk proofs execute legitimate
transitions from the exact frozen canonical states:

- pinned producer/consumer from `S_DOM`;
- **INJECTED** M-DOM producer with its own artifact, VK and verifier from
  `S_DOM`;
- pinned producer/consumer from `S_SRC`; and
- unchanged pinned producer/VK/verifier with the **INJECTED** M-SRC consumer
  from `S_SRC`.

All four proofs are accepted by bb.js and the actually deployed generated
verifier. Each actual `mint` succeeds, consumes the expected nullifier, mints
`5e18` to the named recipient, mints zero to the distinct named relayer, and
increases total supply by `5e18`. The zero-fee choice makes these non-vacuity
controls independent of fee arithmetic; it is not a fee-policy result.

The M-DOM artifact is also decoded independently. Its own ABI-derived witness
layout, all 86 Poseidon2 calls, all 77 exact BN254 Poseidon regions, and a
163-term producer inventory are frozen. The comparison with pinned records
every decoded JSON-pointer difference, not only a narrative summary.

This is development-fixture and producer-provenance evidence. Synthetic
`S_DOM` admission is privileged and makes no cross-chain reachability claim.
Passing the controls does not run O1/O2, establish policy correctness,
automatic discovery, a vulnerability, solver capability, performance, or a
D1 verdict.

## 4. Gate 3 — independent M-DOM producer provenance

### 4.1 Own ABI and Poseidon2 mapping

`warptoad_variant_provenance.py` starts from the M-DOM artifact SHA-256 and
decodes it directly. It recursively flattens that artifact's ABI and checks
the names against its decoded public/private parameter sets before inspecting
any call. It does not call the pinned provenance extractor or consume pinned
witness mappings.

The 11 public witnesses remain 0–10. The independently derived private
witnesses relevant to the M-DOM hash boundary are:

- `aztec_merkle_data.leaf_nonce`: witness 58;
- injected `aztec_merkle_data.contract_address`: witness 59; and
- public configured address: witness 5, recorded separately and explicitly
  rejected as the first silo-hash address input.

Every Poseidon2 opcode is classified from M-DOM's own embedded debug stack and
validated against M-DOM's own ABI witnesses:

| construction | calls |
|---|---:|
| siloed note | 1 |
| unique note | 1 |
| depth-42 Merkle left candidate | 42 |
| depth-42 Merkle right candidate | 42 |
| total | 86 |

Each `M-DOM-P2-000` through `M-DOM-P2-085` row records initialization,
ordered absorption roles, exact domain/IV defining constraints, the four
ordered input and output witnesses, lane-0 selection, source stack, and an L1
application of one shared `P2_WIDTH4_SHARED` tuple function. L1 states only
that equal ordered inputs have equal ordered outputs. It adds no injectivity,
collision resistance, preimage resistance, or bijectivity. BN254 Poseidon
arithmetic remains exact.

The 44-versus-86 construction count is re-established on M-DOM evidence:
two top-level hashes plus two compiled orientation candidates for each of 42
Merkle levels, `2 + 42*2 = 86`.
