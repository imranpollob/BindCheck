### 3.1 No invented producer guarantee or effect-identity relation

A central audit correction occurred before the protocol and raw run were
frozen. The existing static analyzer's positive occurrence and
`binding_status=EXACT` records show syntactic proof-enforced use within their
bounded extraction model. The analyzer itself states that occurrence does not
establish cryptographic injectivity or intent. It therefore cannot by itself
establish Step 9's relational authorization guarantee.

Step 10 emits **zero** `producer_guarantee` facts. A caller-linked
authorization relation is exported only as `producer_binding_candidate` with
`MAY`, over-approximation, `ANNOTATED`, and reason
`AUTHORIZATION_RELATION_NOT_DERIVED`. It cannot discharge a `MUST` obligation.
This sacrifices an apparent positive mechanical result rather than silently
turning occurrence or a caller-supplied identity link into authorization.

The structural consumer-use evidence is likewise not enough for this adapter
to prove that a caller-supplied protected-effect identity denotes the exact AST
occurrence. The joined record is therefore `consumer_reliance_candidate` with
`MAY`, over-approximation, `ANNOTATED`, and reason
`PROTECTED_EFFECT_RELATION_NOT_DERIVED`; Step 10 emits zero eligible derived
`consumer_used` facts. Extent facts become
exact conditional facts only where the supported antecedent and a
compiler-backed condition identity exist. Surface-only prefix names,
unjoined disjunctions, dynamic aliases, missing identity links, and unsupported
mappings remain `UNKNOWN`.

## 4. Pre-result identity-chain defect and fix

validator checked referenced fact IDs but did not bind each fact's identities
to the obligation/effect chain. The Step 9 owner fixed this before freezing
Step 9 bytes or generating its raw result.

- producer subject = obligation authorization identity;
- producer object = exact producer/public value identity;
- reliance subject = protected-effect identity;
- reliance object = that same producer/public value identity; and
- the reliance fact must be a source fact of the effect.

Any mismatch makes the identity-chain status and obligation mechanical state
`UNKNOWN`. Step 10 preserves three regressions for the broken pre-result
behavior. All pass. This is a defect/fix record, not an experimental gain.

## 10. Limitations and handoff

This task does not supply the missing relational producer proof. It does not
establish policy correctness, semantic equivalence for arbitrary transforms,
unbounded memory/path soundness, aggregation/splitting/deferral, external-call
semantics, a primary discriminating experiment, E2–E4 evidence, or a paper
claim. Targeted and differential checks do not prove soundness.

Downstream work must preserve these constraints:

1. compatibility work must not convert unresolved decoding, mapping, memory,
   path, or effect semantics into supported facts;
2. a Step 12 protocol must separately preregister how any relational
   `producer_guarantee` and any exact protected-effect identity link will be
   established, not reuse occurrence or a caller map as proof;
3. Step 13 causal validation must keep the exact object/context/value/effect
   chain and must challenge attacker-controlled values with authenticated
   context fixed; and
4. Gate B cannot score this synthetic packet or treat `UNKNOWN` as safe.

The next measurable prerequisite is a hash-bound, independently reviewed
method for deriving at least one `MUST`/`EXACT`/`DERIVED`/`SUPPORTED`
`producer_guarantee` and one `MAY`/conservative/`DERIVED`/`SUPPORTED`
`consumer_used` fact from actual supported compiler/AST semantics, with a
negative paired-witness control. Until then, the common interface correctly
fails closed.
