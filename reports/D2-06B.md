## Same-policy construction

The policy is `SIGMA-WARPTOAD-MINT-V1`, implemented by the pre-existing
`WarpToadPolicy.t.sol` file at SHA-256
`a33732d2262e8115bd4a1ef66cc5f075f5d015cfbe686df14c2fc58862670370`.
Its seven obligations cover:

1. amount and beneficiary/relayer binding;
2. admission of successful giga/local roots;
3. nullifier single use;
4. rollback after rejection or malformed calldata;
5. verifier gating;
6. valid-relation reachability as a positive harness control; and
7. supply delta versus transfer-event accounting.

The harness exercises the actual pinned or M-SRC `WarpToadCore.sol`. Its
`RelationalVerifierStub` compares a proof hash and all eleven public words and
supports rejection; it is not an always-successful verifier. It is also not a
real variant-specific proof. Consequently a failure is an E1 property
counterexample, never E3/E4, and a pass establishes neither global safety nor a
property-specific safe negative.

The smoke configuration was deliberately smaller than a final campaign:
Foundry 1.8.6 (`f63f468b...1f07`), Solc 0.8.29
(`66fabdd1...64d1`), 32 runs, depth 20, one worker, fixed seed
`0x...d206b001`, no network, 300-second case cap, and zero scientific retries.
The construction smoke did not enforce a memory cap. Step 7 must accept or
replace these settings prospectively; it must not treat them as sealed final
configuration.

adapter can execute on two exposed construction inputs. It does not establish

generality, transfer, or Gate A decision. The research verdict remains
