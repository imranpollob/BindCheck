## 4. Per-project analysis

### 4.1 Set A — reserved second attempt

**NullProof.** v1.2 decodes the beta.19 producer: 300 opcodes
(`AssertZero` 268, `RANGE` 28, `BrilligCall` 4) and two public leaves. The one
allowed CLI invocation then stops at `UNSUPPORTED_CONSUMER_AST_REQUIRED`.
Afterward, a non-analysis diagnostic generated the compiler AST and invoked
the frozen extractor once; it fails closed with
`SOLIDITY_VERIFIER_BOUNDARY_COUNT:2`, matching the two proof entry points in
`ComplianceGate`. The analyzer was not rerun.

**privacy-preserving-IP-NFT.** Beta.3 remains the exact intended D1P-35
boundary: its distinct legacy schema was not added. The invocation stops at
the compiler identity before rule evaluation.

**zkt-research.** The beta.21 container now passes the old compiler-version
gate and reaches semantic decoding. It stops on the named unsupported black
box `MultiScalarMul`. No unsupported operation is skipped.

**ZKPassport circuits.** As in D1P-33, the beta.22 outer circuit stops on the
named unsupported `RecursiveAggregation` black box.

### 4.2 Set B — unseen, non-reserved

**zk-panagram.** The beta.21 circuit and Solidity contracts compile. The
consumer verifies the proof at `panagram.sol:65`, records the per-round win at
line 67, and mints either winner or runner-up NFT at lines 71/74. Producer
decoding stops on `BETA22_MEMORY_OPERATION_ENCODING`: beta.21 supplies an
expression-list payload where the delegated beta.22 decoder expects its own
boolean form.

**nachweis-app.** The circuit and contracts compile. `NoirPidVerifier` checks
the four registry-facing public values at lines 89–98 and calls the HONK
verifier at line 100. Producer decoding stops on the same named beta.21
MemoryOp encoding difference before the consumer/rule path.

**confidential-agent-policy-verdicts.** The allowlist, deny and not-allowed
circuits all compile under exact beta.25, and contracts compile. The selected
adapter constructs a 40-word public-input vector at lines 65–78. V1.2 is
frozen only through beta.21, so the invocation rejects the exact beta.25
compiler identity.

**e-votinng-diploma.** The beta.21 circuit and scoped consumer/verifier builds
compile. `Voting.vote` checks election ID/root/replay and commits the
nullifier/vote count at lines 82–97. The producer reaches semantic decoding
and stops on unsupported `MultiScalarMul`.

These source facts are preserved for audit of target selection and stopping
position. They are not warnings produced by BindCheck and are not manually
promoted into findings.
