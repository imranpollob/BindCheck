plus DEV-F35. Fourteen groups are true for their stated source property, and

## 7. Assumptions, limitations, and unexpected observations

- Applicability is established only for recovered supported function paths; it
  is not whole-application policy inference.
- P1 mapping-write recognition is name- and syntax-bounded. Arbitrary storage
  semantics, modifiers, inheritance, complex interprocedural effects, and
  rollback through unsupported external behavior remain `UNKNOWN`.
- P2 cannot infer whether domain separation is required. That is why its
  applicable decided rate remains 3.7% rather than being inflated with passes.
- P5 IC count establishes count, not semantic signal order. Dynamic or opaque
  vectors and renamed meanings remain `UNKNOWN` unless independently bound.
- Ten rows still have no retained triple, one row remains cap-affected, and 31
  circuit builds fail. None is N/A or verified by absence.
- The first diagnostic command used `--skip-fetch` after ignored source
  checkouts had disappeared and stopped before analysis with
  `FileNotFoundError`. The 56 exact DEV revisions were then reacquired and
  validated; no target revision changed.
- Two noncontrolling T24 diagnostic namespaces record intermediate rules:
  `after-20261106T153640Z-15958` and `after-20261106T155252Z-22241`. They are
  preserved raw development evidence and are not the controlling result.
- A shell-only source-review command accidentally assigned zsh's tied `path`
  variable, making `sed` unavailable in that one command. It changed no file;
  the review was repeated with explicit paths.
