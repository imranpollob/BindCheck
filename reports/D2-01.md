## 3. Integrated interface

`src/bindcheck_d1/integrated_analyzer_v1.py` composes:

- the corrected `static_analyzer_v1` result, including `R-EXTENT`;
- optional `R-ROLE` analysis from `role_binding.py` when a producer semantic
  source is routed; and
- explicit `SUPPORTED`, `UNKNOWN`, `NOT_REQUESTED`, and `NOT_RUN` component
  states with named reasons.

Static warnings and role warnings retain their legacy `WNNN` or `ROLENNN`
source IDs and receive a `BC-...` stable ID. The stable identity hashes the
target artifact/consumer identity, rule, and recursively normalized semantic
evidence while excluding warning order and nested source-coordinate metadata.
Semantic fields such as public indices and roles remain identity-bearing.

The validator recomputes stable IDs before selection or route matching and
rejects stale or forged serialized IDs. A plain two-argument
`bindcheck analyze` invocation still emits the historical static schema for
compatibility; integration inputs or explicit `--integrated` select the new
schema.
