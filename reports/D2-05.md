## 3. Comparison design

Five primary result types are fixed:

1. E1-or-higher target detection by eligible case and arm;
2. predeclared semantic diagnosis-field coverage and transformation stability;
3. highest E0–E4 grade plus automatic/assisted/manual validation stages;
4. property-specific safe-control false warnings and separate non-target
   adjudication; and
5. human, agent, machine, setup, specification, scoring, correction,
   validation, adjudication, and elapsed effort.

Step 6a must return producer-only, consumer-only, unjoined union, basic
structural composition, strong simple composition, and full BindCheck. Strong
simple composition and full BindCheck receive the same conditional facts,
identities, policy, annotations, assumptions, and bounds. Step 6a must also
construct seeded renaming, helper-extraction, equivalent-expression, and
matched-reordering controls, preserving rejected attempts and construction
failures.

Step 6b must return reviewed policies and same-policy harnesses, pinned Noir
default checks, and a source-backed native/adapted/unsupported/unavailable/
not-applicable inventory for applicable circuit, contract, closest-work, and
optional model baselines. Every tool with a property interface gets a
generic/default and same-policy arm. Frontend incompatibility is not a miss,
and an optional LLM arm cannot block the comparison.

## 4. Labels, policies, controls, and matching

A human policy author derives `sigma` from primary project evidence before
scored output. A different human reviews it. A policy states compatible
identity, asset, beneficiary, domain, quantity, state/version, activation, and
discharge fields where applicable; occurrence and role-name equality alone are
not authorization.

Step 7 freezes structured semantic targets before output. A match covers the
case/revision, producer relation or permitted one-sided locus, decoded identity
or indices, consumer state/effect/obligation locus, property direction, and
minimum evidence level. Multi-report union is allowed only where frozen, and
all required identities/indices must be covered. Warning ordinal and source
line never define finding identity. Normalization retains missing locations and
diagnoses rather than inventing them.

Two humans independently score genuinely ambiguous localization, diagnosis,
and non-target records from randomized tool-neutral packets, with a distinct
third adjudicator after originals are sealed. AI is not a scorer, policy
reviewer, or adjudicator. Step 7 may restrict objective scoring to rows with
fully frozen policy and target fields: unresolved case-specific policy or
human scoring makes only the affected row `UNRESOLVED`, not unrelated rows.

Payy check-added is a repair contrast for the exact tail property, not global
safety. Pinned WarpToad is an honest matching development control, not a
proven negative. A false-warning denominator exists only for a control with a
positive property-specific semantic/proof/execution justification.

## 5. Denominators, uncertainty, and resources

Construction, applicability, target detection, diagnosis, safe controls,
non-target reports, validation candidates, transformations, and effort have
separate denominators. Unsupported, unresolved, not-applicable, and
build-failed rows remain visible but never become misses. Unique candidates,
not warning objects, define the validation denominator.

The design is a purposive exposed comparison over two lineages. It reports
exact tables and missingness, not confidence intervals, p-values, pooled
precision/recall, or prevalence. Three frozen seeds are draft-required for
stochastic tools, but repeats are not independent cases.

Draft per-case/arm caps are 1,800 seconds for deterministic, static, and
symbolic arms and 3,600 seconds aggregate for same-policy or stochastic arms.
Per unique candidate, E2/E3/E4 receive 1,800/3,600/1,800 seconds. The draft
memory cap is 16,384 MiB per process, scored parallelism one, network calls
zero, and scientific retries zero. Construction is capped at 24 researcher-
hours and scoring/adjudication at eight. Step 7 must accept or prospectively
amend these draft caps and record an enforcement method before sealing.

One infrastructure recovery is possible only before scored output, under
identical sealed bytes/configuration, in a separately named namespace that
retains the failed attempt. Setup and proving are measured separately from the
analysis cap but remain in total cost. Manual-step count is a proxy and never
claimed as time saved.

## 6. Step 7 and decision rule

Step 7 owns the complete timestamped manifest and content seal. It must bind
actual cases, lineages, exposures, files/hashes, labels, policies, controls,
failures, transformations, arms, information budgets, tools/configurations,
seeds, forms, human roles, validation routes, resources, retries, output
namespace, and completion checks before any scored execution.

Gate A has three outcomes:

- `SUPPORTED_ADVANTAGE`: a valid, transformation-stable relationship
  diagnosis or higher grade appears for full BindCheck but not strong simple
  composition or any applicable equal-information comparator. This supports
  only a bounded exposed-case statement.
- `INCONCLUSIVE`: policies, controls, cases, credible arms, scoring, evidence,
  or information parity do not permit the intended distinction.
- `CURRENT_CLAIM_CONTRADICTED`: an equal-information simple/applicable method
  matches or exceeds full BindCheck on every eligible relational target, or
  the apparent advantage depends on bug-specific information or fails
  controls.

There is no arbitrary two-defect threshold and no automatic SoK pivot. A
Steps 8–10 prototype requires one concrete generalizable missing mechanism,
one frozen test and controls, explicit success/failure criteria, and a maximum
two researcher-day/eight machine-hour budget before editing. It cannot
retroactively rescore Gate A.
