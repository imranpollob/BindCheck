## 1. Objective and outcome

This task inventories the approved exposed-development population and separates
population candidates, authored target variants, synthetic controls,
historical analysis attempts, unique projects, and architectural families. It
makes no analyzer change and performs no reserved or private discovery.

The bounded eligible population contains **three pinned application revisions,
two repositories, and two code/architectural lineages**:

- one WarpToad revision;
- the Payy earlier and check-added revisions, which are one before/after
  lineage rather than two independent families.

All three exact revision candidates are `ANALYZABLE` under the current
interface. This status means the requested analysis completes far enough to
produce supported facts or warnings; it is not a security verdict. Within
this bounded census, observed `UNAVAILABLE`, `INELIGIBLE`, `BUILD_FAILED`, and
`UNSUPPORTED` candidate counts are zero. Because broader discovery was
prohibited, those zeros are not ecosystem prevalence estimates.

## 2. Boundary and method

The development boundary permits exposed WarpToad, exposed Payy, and authored
synthetic controls. Tracked historical reports may be read only to reconcile
already published counts and limits. No reserved identity, source, label,
evaluator output, or ignored reserved workspace was opened, copied, or
reconstructed.

The inventory uses five mutually exclusive compatibility statuses:

- `UNAVAILABLE`: an approved identity lacks accessible pinned source or
  required artifact evidence;
- `INELIGIBLE`: no scoped Noir/ACIR producer to Solidity/EVM consumer boundary;
- `BUILD_FAILED`: an eligible attempt obtains no usable producer and consumer
  build route;
- `UNSUPPORTED`: usable source/build evidence reaches a named fail-closed
  analysis gate; and
- `ANALYZABLE`: the requested interface completes far enough to return
  supported facts or warnings.

A failed default build with a successful scoped build is not `BUILD_FAILED`.
`UNSUPPORTED`, silence, and `ANALYZABLE` are compatibility states, not
`VIOLATION` or `VERIFIED_WITHIN_SCOPE` research verdicts.

## 7. Barrier ranking and recommendation

Only exposed-development rows with known lineage can be family-ranked:

| rank | barrier | affected lineages | affected variants | risk |
|---:|---|---:|---:|---|
| 1 | named consumer-AST relation stop | 2 | 13 | medium-high |
| 2 | artifact-specific recursive mapping for Payy mutant bytes | 1 | 12 | high |

The mapping row is nested inside the same 12 Payy variants and is not additive.
A sound extension must derive and certify mappings against each mutated
artifact and reject hash mismatch. Reusing either pinned certificate would be
unsound.

The bounded recommendation is to preserve fail-closed behavior, specify and
test the current unsupported consumer-AST shapes, and require artifact-bound
mapping before calling a Payy mutant analyzable. This would deepen one known
lineage, not add 12 families.

The historical beta.3, beta.25, MemoryOp, MultiScalarMul,
RecursiveAggregation, and multiple-consumer-boundary observations remain
implementation-risk context only. Without permitted lineage adjudication they
cannot support a family-ranked frontend priority. No universal target-family
threshold and no claim that a new frontend automatically produces successful
analyses are justified.

## 9. Limits

This task is an audit of already exposed development evidence, not a census of
the wider Noir/EVM ecosystem. It establishes neither unbiased sampling,
supported held-out transfer, prevalence, warning precision, vulnerability,
security, nor venue readiness. It invents no missing metadata and no family
count for the historical projects. `UNKNOWN` remains the research verdict.
