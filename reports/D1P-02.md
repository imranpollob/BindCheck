## What this establishes—and what it does not

This task establishes local byte reproducibility of the six historical v5 SMT
queries from four commit-bound inputs using the exact frozen parser, encoder,
protocol, and specification identities. It closes D1P-01's local artifact
availability blocker.

It does **not** independently prove the parser, cone selection, ACIR encoding,
or compact semantic specification sound. Query agreement is generated by the
same frozen implementation family as the historical records. It does not
establish satisfiability, unsatisfiability, proof feasibility, reachable
consumer execution, committed effects, authorization, coverage, generality,
or a paper contribution. The six historical solver executions remain
timeouts and therefore `UNKNOWN`. The older v3-derived goal-only timeout and
the later v5 specification-only SAT diagnostics remain distinct and unchanged.

Payy and its N=8 variants remain development cases. The available real-proof
consumer replay artifacts operate at the original N=1000 scale and are a
different evidence grade. No solver binary or platform timing is implicated
by this serialization-only result.
