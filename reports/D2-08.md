## Delivered interface

`bindcheck_d1.roles.compiler_identity_v1` provides:

- separate `ObjectIdentity` and `ContextIdentity` values;
- separately hashed `CompilerProvenance` and `IdentityRecord` evidence;
- `IdentityFact` with exact opaque subject/object references, structured
  condition, quantifier, approximation, derivation, support, and provenance;
- first-class `MappingEntry` and `MappingCertificate` values; and
- `IdentityBundle`, `identity_reference`, `validate_bundle`, and
  `validate_mapping_certificate`.

The exact opaque reference is `{schema, semantic_hash,
canonical_bytes_sha256}`. Identifier spelling, source line/path, display label,
warning ordinal/text, artifact hash, and physical ABI index are excluded from
the object/context semantic hash. Those observations remain in separately
hashed provenance.

Bundle replay reconstructs every identity and fact, rejects duplicate fact
IDs, validates internal parent links, and requires explicit external reference
namespaces/inventories. Mapping replay binds exact from/to references
exhaustively to both bundles and checks unique source/target identities and ABI
indices.
