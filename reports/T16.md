**How `agg_final` consumes an `agg_agg` proof (source, `10_source_evidence.txt`):** `AggFinal::new(proof: AggAggProof)` holds exactly one proof (`pkg/zk-primitives/src/agg_final.rs`). `AggFinalInput::from(AggFinal)` (`pkg/zk-circuits/src/circuits/agg_final.rs`) copies `proof.public_inputs.{old_root, new_root, commit_hash, messages}` and `proof.proof.to_fields()` **verbatim** — no field is recomputed — and hardcodes `verification_key_hash = AGG_AGG_VERIFICATION_KEY_HASH`. The circuit itself (`noir/agg_final/src/main.nr`) asserts that hash, rebuilds the 1005-field `agg_agg` public-input array from the same values, and calls `verify_proof_with_type` on the supplied `agg_agg` proof and verification key — i.e. a recursive proof check, not a re-derivation of the messages. **Acceptance by `agg_final` was not assumed** from `agg_agg`'s own verification; it is tested directly below by giving the recursive proof to the circuit and observing whether its witness is satisfiable and whether the resulting proof itself verifies independently.

**Harness:** `payy-harness/t16_tail_to_agg_final.rs`, one new untracked file in the revision-A worktree; no circuit, key, or upstream source is touched. It reads the two files above, concatenates `public_inputs ++ proof` and recovers the structured `AggAggProof` with the existing `Proof::from_raw_proof_bytes` split (the same byte boundary the files were already split at when written in T15 — nothing is computed), asserts the recovered proof bytes equal the original file byte for byte, then calls `AggFinal::new(..).prove(b)`.

## 4. What this establishes at each layer

- **`agg_agg` witness/proof (established in T15, not repeated here):** at the earlier revision, a message word outside the active sequence is unconstrained; the synthetic tail record is accepted at the witness level and an ordinary `agg_agg` proof of it verifies under that revision's own key.
- **`agg_final` witness/proof and public messages (this task):** the same proof, unmodified, is accepted as a valid recursive input by `agg_final` at the earlier revision. The resulting `agg_final` proof independently verifies, and the synthetic record appears, unaltered, at the predicted outer public-input indices 7–11, alongside an unchanged root and commit hash. This shows the unconstrained word **propagates through one more proof layer without being re-checked or stripped** — `agg_final` rebuilds the `agg_agg` public inputs from the given values and verifies the recursive proof; it does not independently re-derive or bound the message contents.
- **Consumer behavior — not tested here.** Nothing about `RollupV1.verifyRollup`, `verifyAllMessages`, `verifyBurn`, validator signatures, who could submit such a block, or any effect on balances or state was run, examined again, or assumed in this task. Successful `agg_final` verification shows proof propagation through the circuits; it does not show that a consumer would accept, execute, or be affected by such a block, and no claim of exploitability, loss, or vulnerability status is made.
