## 3. Runner repair

The new runner keeps D1P-05's `libproc` process-tree RSS sampling but replaces
the launch/exit/cleanup path:

1. It polls the child before each sample.
2. If the root is absent from a sample, it calls bounded `wait()` for 0.25
   seconds. Only a confirmed and reaped exit becomes normal completion.
3. A still-live root or exit-confirmation error is a genuine monitoring
   failure and triggers bounded cleanup.
4. Cleanup catches `PermissionError`, `ProcessLookupError` and other errors,
   attempts process-group `SIGKILL`, waits at most 0.25 seconds, then attempts
   direct child kill and waits at most another 0.25 seconds. It never waits
   indefinitely.
5. Cleanup errors, fallbacks and reap status are structured data. Any cleanup
   error stops a campaign even if fallback reaps the child.
6. Every path writes stdout, stderr and call JSON. The campaign catches runner
   exceptions and writes a full ledger with remaining calls marked unrun.
7. Missing RSS observations are JSON null and `memory_observation_available`
   is false; zero is never invented.

The repaired path was exercised naturally on fresh call 4,
goal-only `check_added/prefix`: the sample lost the just-exited root, bounded
wait confirmed exit 0, SAT and its witness were preserved, and the call
completed normally in 3.065210 seconds. D1P-06 failed at this same schedule
position. No production D1P-07 call reported a monitor or cleanup error.
