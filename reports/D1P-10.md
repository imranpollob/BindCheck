## 3. Preservation arguments and validation

### 3.1 Exact radix candidate

The alternative replaces a 32-bit binary expansion with base-`B` digits for
`B` in 4, 16, or 256.  For every digit `d`, the equation
`product(d-j, j=0..B-1)=0` has exactly the distinct field roots
`0..B-1`.  Because the pinned prime exceeds `2^32`, the reconstruction
`x=sum(B^i*d_i)` neither wraps nor aliases a value at or above `2^32`.
Ordinary radix expansion proves the converse.  One-bit ranges retain the
binary root.  This is the universal algebraic preservation argument; the
solver controls were supplemental exact-serialization regressions, not a
finite-sample proof.

The frozen execution nevertheless required all four selected-candidate
boundary checks to complete before benchmarking.  Each radix-4 boundary call
timed out at 30 seconds:

| Value | Expected | Result | Seconds | Peak sampled RSS |
| --- | --- | --- | ---: | ---: |
| `0` | SAT | UNKNOWN / timeout | 30.056575 | 550,420,480 B |
| `2^32-1` | SAT | UNKNOWN / timeout | 30.044182 | 543,227,904 B |
| `2^32` | UNSAT | UNKNOWN / timeout | 30.021876 | 547,078,144 B |
| `p-1` | UNSAT | UNKNOWN / timeout | 30.074847 | 505,708,544 B |

Thus the exact serialized alternative failed the frozen pre-main validation
gate and was not benchmarked.  The failure does not refute the algebraic
argument; it leaves this implementation unvalidated under its declared
solver-based gate.

### 3.2 Conservative propagation

The propagated arm retains every complete producer-cell predicate, all 58
original ranges, every original goal, and explicit equalities for every
eliminated witness.  Initial constants are only the D1P-09-enumerated cell
implications: inactive activation equals zero and a length-4 first word equals
two.  A nonzero activation is never changed to one, no disjunction member is
selected, and no payload is fixed.

Exact substitution preserves each polynomial.  A unique affine equation over
the prime field is replaced only with its unique reconstruction equality.
Known-index reads are replaced only after checking the initialization and all
preceding write indices; symbolic suffixes remain.  Free extensions remain
declared and arbitrary.  This is the general preservation argument for the
implemented rule set.

The T22 regression comparing original `w9=w1` under `w1=7` with retained
reconstruction `w9=7` returned UNSAT in 0.123511 seconds.  The 25 exact
whole-cone XOR validations then produced:

- 12 UNSAT in 0.235126–0.242485 seconds; and
- 13 UNKNOWN/timeouts in 30.023759–30.079012 seconds.

The completed cells were `06..09`, `16..19`, and `21..24`.  Every cell with a
left `inactive` or left `length_4` predicate timed out, as did cells `05`,
`15`, and `20` whose right predicate is `inactive`.  This correlation is an
observation, not a cause analysis or a claim about range bottlenecks.  Because
the protocol required all 25 XOR checks UNSAT, the arm was ineligible and no
propagated primary cell ran.

## 8. Recommendation, limitations, and next task

Do not adopt radix 4 or the new propagated-cell serialization as a shared
backend improvement on this evidence.  Profiling produced no resolved result;
the exact validation gates failed; and the primary comparison could not
measure either transformed arm.  The unchanged baseline also resolved none of
the four obligations.  This is a null feasibility result, not evidence that
the encodings are inequivalent, unsafe, or slower, and not evidence that range
constraints are the bottleneck.

The selected-cone boundary remains a fundamental limitation.  Nothing here
establishes full-ACIR satisfiability, recursive proof feasibility, exact N=8
forwarding/key correspondence, successful consumer execution, authorization,
coverage, or a D1 verdict.  A timeout is never safe.  A SAT result, had one
occurred, would still have required the original selected-cone/case/goal
assignment check and would not have been full-ACIR or proof evidence.

**Recommended next task:** a planner decision on retiring further selected-cone
encoding tuning.  If one final prerequisite task is authorized, make it a
solver-independent proof-certificate checker for the exact 25 propagation
transformations and the selected radix serialization, with the measurable
completion criterion that all 25 cells and all range widths validate without
cvc5 and without changing any D1P-10 primary input.  Do not start another
solver campaign unless that gate passes.  Forwarding reconstruction, the full
joint encoder, proof build, consumer replay, and held-out evaluation remain
paused.
