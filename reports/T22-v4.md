## Minimized blocker

The [minimizer](../scripts/t22_v4_minimize.py) retained the saved v3
non-vacuity assertion verbatim, removed all 2,242 ACIR/encoding assertions,
and kept only the declarations/definitions needed transitively by that goal.
The saved `min_nonvacuous_goal.smt2` is **22,516 bytes, 205 lines, 18
declarations, 182 definitions and one assertion**, down from 724 KiB and
2,243 assertions. It still takes 300 seconds without an answer. The
[provenance audit](../scripts/t22_v4_validate.py) exited 0 and confirmed that
the retained goal is the original final assertion, every retained line is
byte-identical, and the input/output hashes match. Its output is
`30_blocker_audit.json`.

Short-limit search retained prefixes of 2,000, 1,500, 1,000, 500 and 250
original assertions at 10 seconds, and prefixes of 0, 1, 10, 50, 100, 150
and 200 at 5 seconds. All timed out at those short limits; those exploratory
timeouts were **not** counted as 300-second blockers. Only the zero-prefix
candidate was confirmed twice at 300 seconds. The saved script and metadata
are `min_nonvacuous_goal.smt2` (SHA-256
`2a0e539ee0b0dc2cf0df5e724efd176cfb9d37318336ee28c856f7a6c1c0743d`)
and `min_nonvacuous_goal.smt2.json` (SHA-256
`badb5d497a3d37c940b9f0ec392b5ca52e31b4abd5265cab6d2b49fac5c592a7`).

The first confirmation used the minimizer's Python timeout (300.028 s; its

The corrected [`wait4` measurement](../scripts/t22_v4_memory.py) recorded

## Exact specialization and model audit

The [concrete-branch script](../scripts/t22_v4c_concrete.py) fixed stream 0
active with words `[2,0,0,0,0,0,0,0]`, and stream 1 inactive with the same
recursively selected public words. This is a search restriction, not a
protocol-policy assertion. Under the unchanged `walk` derivation, kind 2
emits the first four words, so `L=4`. V3's exact polynomial propagation found
502 implied constants. It reduced the original selected 620-opcode cone to

cvc5 returned SAT in 79.917 seconds, exit 0, with no relevant unmodeled
black-box outputs in the original cone. The SMT SHA-256 is
`61a2a77ace16edd24c787dd7f9ee1167f653488c33ab938452258e59d30cdefe`.

The frozen script's display parser reported nine numbers because it matched
the first constant term as well as its value. The raw solver `get-value`
response contains exactly eight message values. The separate
[model audit](../scripts/t22_v4c_audit.py) exited 0, parsed the value side of
each pair, found `[2,0,0,0,0,0,0,0]`, independently replayed the walk to
`[2,0,0,0]`, and checked zero unmodeled black-box outputs in the original
cone. It is saved as `40_concrete_audit.json`. The frozen script and its raw
record are unchanged. This SAT remains conditional on satisfiability of
components outside the original cone and says nothing about actual recursive
proofs or committed consumer effects.

## Boolean-rank follow-up

The [v4b script](../scripts/t22_v4b_boolrank.py) kept the v3 ACIR cone and
constraint encoder but represented the specification-side emitted-word count
as one-hot Boolean ranks, with an overflow bucket. At most 16 words can be
emitted in N=8, so this avoids finite-field wrap in the specification count.
It issued the same three general obligations under the same limits. All three
SMT queries used 620 selected ACIR opcodes and 540 witnesses, and every
solver child timed out with exit `-9`, empty stdout/stderr. The driver exited
0 because it recorded these outcomes. `boolrank/results.json` (SHA-256
`e9dedc82adbce5888e2a4058426a3668ea1a3e513554fad8b67315dddc448880`)
contains per-query solver records; the three SMT hashes are recorded there.
The Boolean rewrite was not independently proved equivalent, and no verdict
depends on treating its timeouts as a semantic result.

## Assumptions, limits and next task

The experiment is in-sample and producer-side. The specification's semantic
roles and walk rule are analyst assumptions; the selected ACIR components
assume omitted components satisfiable, and recursive-proof feasibility is
not validated. The fixed-branch SAT is a model-level result with no actual
proof or committed local effect. A timeout never establishes safety.

**Recommended next bounded task:** derive a verified, ACIR-backed summary of
the `messages_index`/walk relation and tail guard for this N=8 case, with an
explicit equivalence or subset-certificate check against the unchanged ACIR.
Then require non-vacuity SAT and prefix/tail UNSAT under 300 seconds each on
the check-added case **and** the running-flag negative control. If that gate
fails, preserve the smallest remaining ACIR-bearing timeout or unsupported
component. Do not tune on the independent transfer case before those controls
are resolved.
