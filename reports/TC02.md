# TC02 — Real inner-proof vector for PY-C02 (Rust stack, beyond E1)

Date: 2026-10-28 (lane-x-muse). Status: DONE (E3 for PY-C02 W001/W002).

## Objective

The v1.1 table left all PY-C02 circuit rows at E1 for lack of a real
inner-proof vector. Produce one with the pinned Rust stack: prove that the
mutant circuit accepts attacker public messages no inner proof stands
behind, verify under the mutant's own key, and contrast against pristine
pinned revision A (expected: rejection at the removed export assertion).

## What was built / changed (tracked)

- `payy-harness/tc02_tail.rs` (NEW, tracked): modelled on T15's harness.
  Real utxo → agg_utxo → agg_agg chain, then three attempts on the same
  inner proofs: control (honest 4 mint words), candidate_tail (T15-style
  burn record @4–8), candidate_active (attacker mint record @0–3 with a
  synthetic hash no proof stands behind).
- Ignored workdirs: `build/TC02/{case,case-pinned,crs,vk-mutant,
  capture-mutant2,capture-pinned}` (checkouts, keys, bb captures),
  `results/raw/TC02/` (logs, limiter JSON, harness.json outputs,
  tampered inputs). Cargo target dirs were deleted after use (disk).

| attempt | mutant | pinned (pristine, committed key) |
|---|---|---|

## Assumptions and limitations

- E3 grades the circuit defect (real proof + verified under mutant VK).
  E4 (consumer replay with the real verifier + validator-signed block)
  is future work for all Payy rows.
- W003 (commit_hash R-CONSUMER) is by-design offchain (Celestia): E1, no
  experiment. No held-out claim; D1 verdict UNKNOWN.
- Incidents: (1) first active hash `0xabab..ab` exceeded the BN254 modulus
  and ACVM reduced it mod p — diagnosed from captured public inputs,
  fixed to a low-byte pattern, re-ran clean; (2) two cargo target dirs
  filled the disk to 100% and failed the first pinned build with
  `rustc-LLVM ERROR: No space left on device` — deleted both regenerable
  dirs (mutant evidence already captured), rebuilt pinned with 9.1 G free
  (590.5 s, exit 0).

## Failed approaches

- `nargo execute` + `bb prove` without inner proofs: blocked at
  `verify_proof_with_type` (type 7) — needs valid inner proofs.
- Pinned `Prover.toml` is agg_final-shaped; no agg_agg vectors. bb-3.0.0
  bundle lacks `proof_as_fields`/`vk_as_fields`; key_fields regenerated
  by chunking the vk bytes (verified identical on the committed key).
