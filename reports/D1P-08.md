## 3. Implemented component

`src/bindcheck_d1/symbolic_consumer_v2.py` supplies an inspectable expression
DAG with a configurable message bound. It evaluates expressions on concrete
assignments but has no solver/process-launch path. It reuses:

- `consumer_execution_v1.Token` for the restricted ordinary-token model;
- `consumer_scope_v2.assess` for the funded standard-token/no-substitution V2
  admission boundary;
- `model_v2` artifacts, provenance, keys, policies, and occurrences; and
- `policy_v2.assess_trace` for separate P/O/E, exact occurrence multiplicity,
  and injective authorization/coverage matching.

The transition models the exact configurable length, three-word header,
kind-0/2/3 widths, calldata bounds, sequential mint and token state, low-160
recipient conversion, failed transfer behavior, validator-set state, verifier
and height guards, and atomic outer rollback. Zero-value mint spent-writes,
zero-value burns, and self transfers remain protected occurrences where the
source/model says they commit. Unsupported substitutions, token targets,
boundary facts, types, or configurations return `UNKNOWN` rather than safety.

Positive guard assignments require a stated basis, validator-set post-state,
and attacker capabilities. Verifier evidence records the exact key, verifier,
proof hash, full ordered public-input vector and hash, relation identity and
scope. Proof soundness is explicit: acceptance under the bound key/vector is
assumed to imply a satisfying witness for the named full producer relation.
The historical selected cone is marked as an abstraction and cannot be
silently substituted for that relation.
