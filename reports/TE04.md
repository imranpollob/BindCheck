# TE04 — E4 for PY-C02 W002 with zero mock components

Date: 2026-10-29 (lane-x-muse). Status: DONE (PY-C02 W001/W002 E3→E4).

## Objective

Close the at-minimum-PY-C02 requirement at E4: take the TC02
tail-candidate (forged kind-3 burn @ messages 4–8, no inner proof behind
it) through a mutant-keyed agg_final, verify it onchain with the real
bb-generated Solidity verifier against the pinned Payy consumer, and show
unauthorized token movement on state. Contrast: the same block under the
pinned-keyed verifier must be rejected. No MockVerifier anywhere on the
proof path (the only mock in the stack is MockUSDC, the synthetic asset).

## Assumptions and limitations

- Honesty note: the part-1/part-2 runs executed at default priority
  (oversight against the lane's nice-19 rule); conditions (load ~5.6,
  serial, no competing lane provers) are recorded in AUTO-VALIDATE, and
  the proving step was re-run under `nice -n 19` and verifies.
- E4 scope: unauthorized effect = 7-unit payout on a burning record with
  no backing note/proof; attacker cost zero (the 100-unit deposit belongs
  to the honest mint). No live network, no real keys (anvil + synthetic
  validator key configured at `initialize`), no held-out claim.
- Debugging trail: TOML hex values must be quoted (bare `0x` parses as
  i64); `bb prove` for recursive circuits needs `-k`; the witness is
  `target/agg_final.gz`; solc via-IR fails stack-too-deep on the
  verifier (legacy runs=1); runs=200 output (24.9 KB) exceeds EIP-170 —
  size-optimized instead of unlimited-size chains; the consumer must be
  initialized with the proof's own note-kind.

## Failed approaches

- `write_solidity_verifier` against a non-keccak VK: `val.on_curve()`
  assertion — regenerating the key with `--oracle_hash keccak` (the
  repo's generator flags) fixed it.
- Single-compiler build: bb verifiers require `>=0.8.21`, consumer pins
  `0.8.20` — split compilation, both recorded.
