## 2. Evidence audited

The prior v0.2.0 protocol correctly separates design, reservation, and
annotation completion. Its seed corpus ledger contains 27 findings, 23
families, 23 exposure events, zero annotation rounds, and zero agreement
reports. Lane X's AI pass suggested 19 includes. A later repository-author
sheet contained 17 human includes; the author/AI comparison reported 25/27
relevance agreement and 9/17 single-cause agreement.

Those numbers remain historical exposed-development observations. They are
not inter-human agreement because one side is AI. The human sheet retained
AI-written summaries, used only one seed collection, did not open the linked
primary records, and received a stop-at-first-match precedence instruction
absent from protocol v0.2.0. All 23 read families remain exposed.

The audit identifies two substantive ambiguities:

1. **cause versus consequence:** duplicate claims, wrong paths, and wrong
   effects were sometimes used as causes even when the evidenced root was a
   representation or dependency defect; and
2. **overlapping causes:** range, mapping, activation, occurrence, and generic
   relation-gap codes could describe the same omission, letting instruction
   order determine the result.

CF-04/CF-05 additionally exposed a relevance ambiguity: missing accepted-
transition/effect evidence had been treated as exclusion. The revision makes
missing primary boundary evidence `unresolved`; exclusion now needs positive
evidence of an out-of-scope, pure, or availability-only mechanism.

## 3. Frozen codebook v0.3.0

The controlling machine protocol is
`protocols/d2/d2-04-taxonomy-preparation-v1.json`, status
`FROZEN_PRE_ANNOTATION`. The prose codebook is
`docs/taxonomy/d2-codebook-v0.3.0.md`.

The unit is one root mechanism, affected boundary, and repair lineage. The
primary agreement sample permits at most one scored finding per conservative
family. Relevance is `include`, `exclude`, or `unresolved`. Cause,
consequence, and minimum-sufficient attacker role are independent multilabel
axes whose per-category states are `present`, `absent`, `uncertain`, or
`unassessed`. Missing/null/uncertain/unassessed never means absent.

The cause rule is now explicit:

- label evidenced root boundary mechanisms, not downstream manifestations;
- use multiple causes only for distinct evidenced mechanisms;
- a specific mapping, representation, activation, context, verification-
  configuration, occurrence, or transformation code takes precedence over
  generic `C_RELATION_GAP` for the same relation;
- an out-of-range/non-binary selector is representation unless a second
  activation/mapping relation is independently wrong; and
- replay is a consequence unless the boundary itself loses multiplicity or
  single-use accounting.

Every complete included finding must have `K_AUTHORIZATION`, `K_COVERAGE`, or
both. A reverted effect is not committed. Unknown feasibility remains
uncertain, not safe.

The prior CF-01–CF-27 entries were not relabeled. The separate exposed-pilot
audit records which design issue prompted each revision without predicting
how a prior item would be rated.

## 4. Future corpus and blank artifacts

`docs/taxonomy/d2-sampling-and-annotation-v1.md` requires a complete frozen
source-class frame (or prospective amendment), report/index completeness,
canonical-finding deduplication, conservative family grouping, and a sample or
census rule frozen before annotation. Selection cannot depend on expected
mechanism, analyzer support, E-grade, severity, or a desired coefficient. The
17 human-included Lane-X items can only support a separately named exposed-
pilot study.

The primary-evidence packet separates relation/configuration, decoding and
consumer use, independent policy, actor/reachability/proof/effect evidence,
repair lineage, and missing evidence. It prohibits analyzer output, AI
suggestions, prior labels, other-rater judgments, adjudication, and project
evidence grades. Missing primary evidence is recorded rather than filled from
an aggregator.

The blank annotation form contains all categories with null values. It
contains no judgment or prediction. Two humans work independently and disclose
actual prior exposure. A distinct third human adjudicates only after original
rating files and agreement output are sealed; adjudication appends a cited
resolution and never overwrites either rating.

## 5. Agreement statistic and missingness

Exactly two fixed human raters make Cohen's kappa the predeclared chance-
corrected statistic:

- three-category nominal kappa for relevance; and
- one binary present/absent kappa for every cause, consequence, and role.

For every category the script also reports raw agreement, both marginal
positive prevalences, positive agreement, negative agreement, comparable
denominator, and missing count. These expose low-prevalence behavior rather
than offering alternative coefficients to choose post hoc. Raw exact-set
agreement per complete axis is secondary. No macro kappa is produced.

`uncertain`, `unassessed`, omitted, and null ratings are excluded as missing.
When no comparable pairs or no chance variance exists, kappa is null with a
reason; an all-negative category is not reported as perfect kappa.

No confidence interval is implemented because the future sample size and
independent-family count do not exist. Any interval requires a prospective
amendment fixing a family-cluster design, seed, adequacy rule, undefined-
replicate handling, and interpretation before ratings are unsealed. This is
preferable to an unjustified item bootstrap.

Agreement would measure rating reproducibility, not truth, taxonomy validity,
tool accuracy, security, or ecosystem prevalence.

## 6. Implementation and controls

`scripts/d2_taxonomy_agreement.py` is standard-library-only. It requires
exactly two distinct human raters, disclosed exposure, one scored item per
family, both ratings per item, and the frozen label vocabulary. A
`fresh_independent` input fails closed if either rater was previously exposed.
Inputs containing adjudication are rejected. CLI results bind the ratings file
and protocol by SHA-256 and refuse to overwrite output.

Known-answer controls cover:

- three-category relevance kappa `7/11` from a four-item table;
- binary kappa `0`, raw/positive/negative agreement `0.5`;
- null kappa for an all-absent, no-chance-variance category;
- missing values not coerced to absent;
- exact-set agreement `0.5`;
- rejection of AI raters, duplicate families, exposed items in a fresh round,
  and adjudication mixed with original ratings; and
- blank templates containing no judgments.

python3 -m json.tool protocols/d2/d2-04-taxonomy-preparation-v1.json
