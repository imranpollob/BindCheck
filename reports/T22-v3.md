### Failure causes and what changed

1. **R1 fixes the observed running-flag probe false positive.** All eight
   message goals specialize to zero and are UNSAT. This agrees with the
   original-ACIR subset certificates in T22 Section 3.2. It does not establish
   the full tail property for an active prefix: general verification remains
   UNKNOWN.
2. **R2/R4 recover the two missed mutant probes.** Re-componentization leaves
   four opcodes and three ACIR witnesses for each constrained comparison slot.
   Testing slots in order finds the unchecked `>`-guard slot 0 and skipped
   final slot 7. Their SAT goals, like the earlier/deleted-helper cases, retain
   zero opcodes and one unconstrained message witness after specialization.
3. **Six general queries time out.** The check-added and running-flag cases
   each time out on `nonvacuous`, `prefix` and `tail` at 300 seconds. Every
   timeout record has `timed_out=true` and solver exit status `-9` from the
   harness's SIGKILL. For the check-added case, all three SMT files are
   byte-identical to v2; changing the solver mode did not resolve them.
4. **R3 exposes nine allocation failures instead of an early abort.** All
   three `agg_utxo` variants refute only the all-streams-inactive premise,
   then issue all three general queries. Each cvc5 call exits 1 with
   `(error "std::bad_alloc")` under the frozen 4 GiB virtual-memory cap,
   without reaching its time limit. These are solver/resource failures, not
   UNSAT, safety results or counterexamples. Their exact causes and exit
   statuses are indexed in `31_query_causes.json`.
5. **R6 retains the black-box warning.** The `agg_utxo` general cones retain
   unmodeled Poseidon outputs, now visible in the case notes. Even if a query
   later returns SAT, those dependencies would prevent a supported violation
   claim until resolved. The allocation failures do not diagnose which part
   of the enlarged encoding caused the memory demand.

The primary solver calls comprise 27 slot queries (4 SAT, 23 UNSAT) and
15 general queries (6 timeouts, 9 allocation failures). Three additional
probe-premise contradictions are derived by exact propagation without issuing
a solver call. No non-empty-prefix non-vacuity query succeeds.
