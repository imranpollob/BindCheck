### 3.4 Connection from the offline partition to emitted SMT

D1P-03's five single-stream classes are emitted directly over the original
activation and eight word witnesses. Inactive requires activation zero.
Active-empty requires nonzero activation and a first word outside producer
kinds 2/3/4. Length 4 requires kind 2 at offset 0 and no valid next kind at
offset 4. Length 5 requires kind 3 or 4 at offset 0 and no valid next kind at
offset 5. Truncated-8 requires a valid second record beginning at offset 4 or
5, so that the producer walk consumes beyond the available eight-word stream.
Their Cartesian product is the emitted 25-cell partition.

For cell lengths `(L0,L1)`, ranks `0..L0-1` select stream-0 offsets in order;
ranks `L0..L0+L1-1` select stream-1 offsets in order. Each rank below eight is
paired with outer witnesses `w1275..w1282`; ranks eight and above are explicitly
recorded as overflow with no outer lookup. `nonvacuous` is true exactly when
the total length is 1..8. `prefix` compares every in-range rank to that exact
payload lookup and becomes true on overflow. `tail` compares outer offsets
from the total length through 7 against zero and becomes false when total
length is at least eight.

The generator's mapping and goals are the SMT transcription of the offline
enumeration, not a fresh semantic assumption. The focused tests independently
check the 25 predicates, rank/offset map, length-eight and overflow edges, and
producer-prefix bytes. SMT equivalence was intended to connect that finite
rewrite evidence to each actual parent walk; because those calls did not run,
the connection remains an argument plus structural tests, not solver-
established equivalence.

## 4. Equivalence construction and attempted validation

Each equivalence query contains the parent's finite-field header, declarations
for the exact original outer/activation/payload witnesses, and the exact
original walk definitions. It excludes every producer assertion. The original
last assertion is converted to a Boolean definition and is **not** asserted as
a premise. The sole assertion is equivalent to:

```text
cell AND common_definitions AND
((original_goal AND NOT specialized_goal) OR
 (specialized_goal AND NOT original_goal))
```

Both directions are therefore checked in one query. The formulas reuse the
original field-sorted witnesses and introduce no unconstrained correspondence
symbols. No additional definitional correspondence is required: both goals
refer directly to the same witnesses and original walk Booleans.
