Date: 28 October 2026  
Task status: **DONE for the bounded development benchmark**

## 1. Outcome

The frozen BindCheck v1 implementation analyzed all 30 binding mutants and
all five D1P-30 targets. It detected the named mutation in **13/30** targets,
recorded **16/30 as unsupported**, and had **one supported miss**. On only the
14 supported targets this is 13/14, but the paper-facing all-target result is
13/30; unsupported targets are never converted into misses or successes.

All 13 detections name the broken semantic public word, effect, anchor, or
replay obligation described in the mutant ground truth. The benchmark
markdown does not record literal source line numbers, so location accuracy is
scored at that semantic boundary location, not as edit-line recovery.

The 30 analyses emit 38 warning objects: 16 are ground-truth-related and 22
are unrelated. All 22 unrelated warnings are valid reports of the known Payy
message-tail defect inherited from its deliberately vulnerable baseline; they
are not detections of the newly named mutations and are not called semantic
false positives. Pinned WarpToad and Payy check-added again emit no warning,
so the bounded D1P-30 negative controls remain **0/2 false warnings**.

All 38 mutant warnings were passed once to `bindcheck validate`, grouped by
operator class. WT-C03 and WT-S01 reuse preserved D1P-30 E4 evidence after
fresh correspondence checks; each route counts two manual inputs. The other
36 warnings have no content-addressed concrete route and remain E1. No new
proof job ran, so the 45-minute proving allowance consumed zero proving
seconds. This is a development benchmark over already exposed applications,
not held-out transfer, prevalence, or a security verdict.

### 4.4 Warning explanations and fixes

Every JSON warning carries its complete explanation and suggested fix. The
rule-level actions used in the per-target rows are:

| rule | diagnostic | suggested repair |
|---|---|---|
| R-BIND | consumer verifier word has zero decoded proof-enforced ACIR occurrences | constrain the exposed word in the producer, or remove it from the effect surface; rebuild artifact/key/verifier together |
| R-EFFECT | an effect amount/beneficiary/message word is absent from proof-enforced ACIR | bind every effect-decoded word and derive effects only from those bound inputs |
| R-ANCHOR | a proof-visible root lacks a must-be-in state check on every success path | require the root in the appropriate current/history mapping before acceptance |
| R-POLARITY | replay protection lacks the absent-key guard or same-key success write | check absence before verification and atomically write the same key on success |

For WT-C03, the benchmark and D1P-15 artifacts have identical decoded
`bytecode`, ABI, and `noir_version`; only artifact metadata differs. For

source. The reused D1P-30 chains already contain a concrete witness, supported
NoirJS ACVM execution (the documented alternative where a complete
`Prover.toml` is unavailable), real proof, generated-verifier acceptance, and
unchanged-consumer state effects. They are not fresh D1P-31 proofs.

The 36 E1 outcomes are validation-route limitations, not evidence that the
warnings are infeasible. No E2-only or E3-only result occurred. Automatic
witness synthesis remains absent.
