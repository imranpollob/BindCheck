## Limits, failed approaches, and next task

The local observations establish committed effects under the stated synthetic setup. They do not establish live deployment, funded exposure, author-approved policy, issuer-backed credentials, or a current BindCheck detection advantage over a strong consumer-only baseline. A proof's public wallet/commitment is not automatically an authenticated person. The patches are scoped design contrasts, not audited production fixes.

The RacePass setup had two `circomlibjs` module-resolution failures ([first](../../results/raw/impact/racepass-impact-20261109T053739Z-34172/20_make_inputs.log), [second](../../results/raw/impact/racepass-impact-20261109T054003Z-39330/20_make_inputs.log)); the runner now pins this harness-only package. A later [compile failure](../../results/raw/impact/racepass-impact-20261109T054204Z-46347/61_repair_compile.log) exposed the repository dependency/compiler conflict. An initial [repair matcher failure](../../results/raw/impact/racepass-impact-20261109T054441Z-55901/62_repair_control.log) was a test-observation mistake: the [diagnostic receipt](../../results/raw/impact/racepass-impact-20261109T054441Z-55901/debug-test-4.log) showed on-chain status `0`; the final control checks static-call reasons and transaction receipts separately. These failed attempts remain `UNKNOWN`, not wins.

**Recommended next bounded task:** obtain advisor-reviewed, explicit issuance and pass multiplicity/holder policies and add issuer-authenticated positive controls. Completion criterion: both policies identify the authorizing source of each effect value; the repaired circuits/contracts reject the corresponding unauthorized witness while accepting a legitimate issuer-backed witness, with the proof, verifier, receipt, and state recorded. Do not call the current patches full remediation before that criterion is met.
