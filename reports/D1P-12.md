## 5. Intended policy, implementation, and hash dependence

### 5.1 Documented/source-supported intent

This paragraph states protocol intent, not an implementation verdict. D1P-11

### 5.2 Source-observed implementation facts

The circuit source enforces the following visible relations
([`main.nr` lines 45–74](https://github.com/warptoad/warp-toad/blob/973e7d1e4b60d0d8a8ca299b0cabd9193b147ef8/backend/circuits/withdraw/src/main.nr#L45-L74)):

- `amount >= max_fee` in the circuit's field/integer comparison semantics;
- `fee_factor != 1`, `priority_fee != 0`, and both beneficiary fields nonzero;
- if `destination_local_root != origin_local_root`, Merkle membership of the
  origin root in `giga_root`;
- `hash_commitment(nullifier_preimage, secret, chain_id, amount)` produces a
  commitment and derived nullifier, with equality to the public `nullifier`;
- the commitment is Merkle-verified against `origin_local_root` using either
  the Aztec path plus `aztec_warptoad_address` or the EVM path.

The exact inner Poseidon/hash formulas and Merkle encodings are in missing
circuit dependency source. D1P-12 therefore records the call-level dependency
without inventing the helper semantics.

The consumer first requires accepted stored roots and an unused nullifier,
marks the nullifier, and calls the verifier. Solidity rollback means a later
revert would undo the early write. For nonzero `fee_factor`, it computes
`relayer_fee = fee_factor * (block.basefee + priority_fee)`, requires the
result not exceed `max_fee`, optionally replaces sentinel `address(1)` with
`msg.sender`, then mints `relayer_fee` and `amount - relayer_fee`. For zero
`fee_factor`, it mints all `amount` to the recipient
([`WarpToadCore.sol` lines 155–176](https://github.com/warptoad/warp-toad/blob/973e7d1e4b60d0d8a8ca299b0cabd9193b147ef8/backend/contracts/core/WarpToadCore.sol#L155-L176)).

### 5.4 Extension needed beyond exact occurrence matching

An eventual joint analyzer would need more than matching one proof occurrence
to one mint occurrence. At minimum it must model or discharge:

1. the actual Poseidon/hash and both Merkle helper relations, including leaf,
   path, index, tree depth and Aztec contract-domain semantics;
2. field canonicality and range obligations across Noir `Field`, Solidity
   `uint256`, `address`, and `bytes32`, rather than equating representations by
   role name;
3. the block/environment binding: `block.chainid` enters the proof statement,
   while `block.basefee` enters only consumer fee arithmetic;
4. checked Solidity addition/multiplication/subtraction and the bounded fee
   split, including the `address(1) → msg.sender` relayer substitution;
5. stored-root initialization/history assumptions and the same-root bypass;
6. nullifier false-to-true state transition, rollback on any revert, and
   repeated-proof rejection;
7. beneficiary policy: whether a source commitment holder may freely choose
   recipient/relayer, and which sentinel substitution is authorized; and
8. configuration/deployment correspondence for `aztecWarptoadAddress`, the
   verifier address, VK and generated verifier.

These are future requirements, not D1P-12 implementation or validation. The
joint analyzer was not changed and no policy verdict is claimed.
