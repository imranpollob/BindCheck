## Delivered interface

The public module is
`src/bindcheck_d1/core/authorization_boundary_v1.py`. It exports versioned
records for policy permissions, authorization obligations, protected effects,
finite queries, common inputs, and per-obligation results. It emits the
mechanical states `ESTABLISHED`, `MISMATCH_CANDIDATE`, and `UNKNOWN`, while
keeping the research-contract verdict separate. Under the fixture policy, the
effective state is always `UNKNOWN`, evidence is E0, and `score_eligible` is
false.

The two named arms, `full-bindcheck` and `strong-simple-composition`, call the
same interpretation on the same canonical input bytes. The equal-arm envelope
records exact input, policy, fact-set, and identity-bundle hashes. No
arm-specific policy, fact, default, or name-derived correspondence exists.

The supported fragment is finite-domain, one-transition, one-to-one, and
occurrence-level. It compares exact action, asset, quantity, beneficiary,
domain, state version, authorizing object/context, and protected-effect
identity. Splitting, aggregation, deferral, unbounded histories, unreviewed
alternative authorization, unsupported conditions, undischarged assumptions,
and unbound mappings remain `UNKNOWN`. The complete semantics and claim limits
are in `docs/formal/d2-09-authorization-boundary-v1.md`.

## Exact identity-chain rule

Step 9 and Step 10 agreed on one chain:

1. a `producer_guarantee` subject is the required authorizing object/context;
2. its object is the exact producer/public value identity;
3. a `consumer_used` subject is the protected effect occurrence; and
4. its object is that same producer/public value identity.

Every link is an exact Step 8 opaque reference containing `schema`,
`semantic_hash`, and `canonical_bytes_sha256`. A different object with the same
display name, the same object in a different context, a swapped effect, or a
different public-value identity yields `UNKNOWN` and cannot discharge an
obligation. Display labels, source names, source locations, and warning text
are not consumed.

Only a `MUST`, `EXACT`, `DERIVED`, `SUPPORTED` producer fact with provenance
can discharge a MUST obligation. A `MAY`/`OVER_APPROXIMATION` consumer fact is
admissible only to conservatively cover possible relying paths. It never
supplies the producer guarantee. MAY producers, underapproximations,
annotations, unknown support, unsupported conditions, unresolved assumptions,
and mapping references without a bound certificate fail closed.

- Finite exhaustive enumeration covers only `{0,1}` for the two attacker
  variables and one fixed authenticated value. It is neither an SMT proof nor
  a theorem about unbounded values.
- The matched control's mechanical `ESTABLISHED` state is not
  `VERIFIED_WITHIN_SCOPE`; policy review, extraction fidelity, concrete
  semantics, proof/replay, reachability, and non-synthetic evidence are absent.

  against a concrete producer, verifier, consumer, or committed effect.
- Exact identity hashes depend on Step 8's declared canonical object/context
  model and its assumptions. This task does not prove compiler or mapping
  soundness.
- The common arm result tests byte parity and shared semantics. It cannot
  distinguish the arms' future extraction or composition capability.
- Human policy roles remain unavailable. The primary experiment, reserved
